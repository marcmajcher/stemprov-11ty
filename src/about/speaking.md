# Conversations that challenge, connect, and inspire action.

I speak about science, democracy, learning, leadership, and collective action, with an emphasis on how we can practice the worlds we want to build together.

Whether I'm delivering a keynote, facilitating a conference session, or joining a panel, my goal is the same: helping audiences leave with new questions, new relationships, and new possibilities for action.

---

## Topics I speak on

### Reimagining Science as a Public Good

How can science become more accountable to the communities it serves?

### Building Belonging in Science

Power, identity, and participation in science communication.

### From Information to Collective Action

What community organizing can teach us about science communication.
### Practicing Democracy

Why participation, creativity, and rehearsal matter for social change.
### Learning Through Play

Improv, Theatre of the Oppressed, and embodied learning for leadership.

### Designing Transformative Learning

How to create workshops people can help us rehearse the cultures we want.
